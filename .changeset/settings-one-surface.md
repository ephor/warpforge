Every Settings section now sits on the same card. Agents and Integrations used to render bare while the rest were boxed, and the lists inside them drew a border around each row on top of that — so the same screen had three different ideas of what a group looks like. Language servers and agent accounts are plain rows separated by a hairline now, inside one surface per section, which also makes the language server list far shorter to scroll.
