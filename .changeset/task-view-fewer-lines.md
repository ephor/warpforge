The task view lost the boxes it did not need. The conversation is frameless now — one rule under its header, at the same height and weight as the workspace tabs beside it, so the two read as a single strip instead of a step. The workspace pane carries the frame, and its tab strip is no longer boxed in by a rule of its own. The composer lost the double outline it drew when focused, along with the line above it, and the title, messages and composer finally share one left edge. In the changes rail, the collapsed commit strip is the button — no outlined button inset inside an outlined footer — and the commit message field is a filled box rather than another bordered one.
