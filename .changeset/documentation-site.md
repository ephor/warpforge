Warpforge has a documentation site. It covers the whole product rather than the quick start: installing it, bringing your own agents and running several logins for each, everything agents can do on your behalf, memory they share across harnesses, choosing between a single agent, an orchestrator and a workflow, writing workflows of your own, the day-to-day craft of working inside a task, the git surface, and a page for every setting.

Its front page opens with Warpforge itself running rather than a screenshot: a real task plays through — a Claude lead handing the test suite to Codex, edits stacking up into a diff you can read line by line, a file tree and a terminal you can click into — and you can view the whole thing in any of the eight themes.
