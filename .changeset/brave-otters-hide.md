The Runtime list is readable again when a project has a lot going on. Start, restart and stop used to sit on every row as a bordered button, so a project with a dozen port-forwards showed a column of identical controls next to the names. Now a row shows just its status and its name, and its controls appear when you point at it, tab to it, or select it — the layout does not shift when they do, and starting or stopping everything at once is still one click in the Services or Port Forwards heading.
