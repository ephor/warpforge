Long model lists are now searchable. Open the model picker in the composer and a search box sits pinned at the top of the list — type to narrow hundreds of models down to the one you want, clear it with the × button, and press Esc to close. Selectors with only a handful of choices stay as simple lists, so nothing extra gets in the way when you just need to switch reasoning effort.
