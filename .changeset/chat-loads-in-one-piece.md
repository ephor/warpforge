Long conversations now open in one piece and stay still. A task's chat used to
arrive in two parts — a recent stretch first, the rest a moment later — which
could repeat messages you had just read, leave blank gaps between tool results,
and drag the view up while you were reading. The whole conversation is now
there the moment the task opens, so the transcript holds its place while an
agent streams, and scrolling up keeps you exactly where you left off.
