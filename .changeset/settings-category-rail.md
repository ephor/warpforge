Settings is organised into categories instead of one long scroll. Open Settings and pick Appearance, Agents, Integrations, Tasks, Memory or Advanced from the list on the left — each shows only its own controls, so finding a theme or a retention window no longer means scrolling past everything else. Warpforge remembers the category you were last on and reopens there, and Escape still closes the whole thing. Every setting you had is still available, just grouped.
