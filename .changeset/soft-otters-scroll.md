The backlog is now one continuous list instead of a paged table. Scroll and the next items load themselves — there are no page buttons to click and no way to end up looking at rows from a page you already left. Each item reads as two lines: title with who it is assigned to and when it last changed, then its status, priority and tracker underneath, with the link out and Start task appearing on hover. Search and the Status/Priority/Source filters work as before and snap the list back to the top, and ordering moved into a Sort control in the toolbar now that there are no column headers to click.
