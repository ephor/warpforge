Scrolling up in a chat now works. While an agent was answering, the transcript dragged you back to the newest message on every token — you could not read what was above, and clicking anything far up threw you to the bottom. Scroll up and you stay there; the jump-to-latest button appears when you want to come back. Long chats no longer show a screen of blank space where messages should be.
