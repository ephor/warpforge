Runtime shows a project's services and port-forwards in one place, with their live logs and the `http://localhost:…` address of anything that is up. A row carries its status and its name, and start, restart and stop appear on it when you point at it; starting or stopping everything at once is a single click in the Services or Port Forwards heading. Whatever you have selected is named in the toolbar itself, so the logs get the height instead. The side panels in Runtime and in the diff view fold away when you want the room.
