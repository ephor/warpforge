Settings rows are readable at a glance. Every setting now states what it does in one line, with the longer explanation — install steps, which file owns the value, what the daemon does about it — moved behind an info icon you can hover when you need it. Controls line up along a shared right edge instead of ragging across the page, and readings the screen cannot change (how many memories you have, which scopes are on, whether dreaming runs) sit in a status line at the foot of their section rather than posing as switches that do nothing when clicked. Task history also spells out the chain it forms — how long before a task settles, loses its chat and is deleted — in a sentence under the three controls.
